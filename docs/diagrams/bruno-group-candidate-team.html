GROUP + CANDIDATE Team Formation & Selection Sequence

Visualizing Candidate Team formation: a Team Leader creates a team, issues a 24-hour Join Code to prospective teammates, submits the completed team application with deliverables, and the Hirer selects the winning team in an all-or-nothing atomic assignment.

BRUNO FOLDERS

1. Squad Formation

  • Quest-Candidate-Teams / Create Team: Team Leader creates squad & receives 24h Join Code.
  • Quest-Candidate-Teams / Join Team: Teammates join with Join Code.
  • Quest-Candidate-Teams / Submit Team: Leader submits team application.

SELECTION PROTOCOL

2. Team-Wide Win

  • Quest-Candidate-Teams / Select Team: Hirer picks the winning squad.
  • All-or-Nothing: All teammates receive ASSIGNMENT_ACTIVE simultaneously, while other squads are rejected.

SUBMISSION PROTOCOL

3. Leader Delivery

  • Team Leader Only: Submits proof on behalf of the squad.
  • Hirer approves → Reward pool released to Leader according to squad agreement.
