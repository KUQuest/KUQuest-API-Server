PromptPay Top-up & Ledger Clearing Sequence

Interactive sequence tracing how a Member converts Thai Baht into integer-satang Spending Balance. Guaranteed by a 300-second binding Provider Quote, Xendit PromptPay payment request, authenticated webhook, and balanced double-entry ledger posting.

1. Binding Provider Quote

The Member requests an integer-satang credit amount. The Server queries the active Money Policy and issues a binding quote with a strict 300-second TTL. The quote locks the exact fee (0.8% PromptPay rate + 7% VAT) and total payment requirement.

2. PromptPay QR & Webhook

Upon confirmation with an Idempotency-Key, KUQuest initiates a Payment Request via Xendit and returns an EMVCo PromptPay QR payload and Data URL. Mobile banking payments trigger an authenticated webhook stored durably in the inbox.

3. Exact-Amount Clearing

The Server verifies the HMAC signature and requires that the received amount equals the quoted total satang. It atomically writes a balanced double-entry ledger transaction crediting the Member's Spending account from Platform Suspense.
