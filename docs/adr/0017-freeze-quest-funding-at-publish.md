# Freeze Quest funding at publish

Publishing a Quest fixes its per-slot Quest Funding Total, its net Quest Reward, its Platform Fee, and its headcount for the life of the Quest. The reserved Quest Escrow therefore remains sufficient for its declared Worker payments and Platform Fees, and later Quest edits do not need to increase or recalculate the Funding Reservation; a Hirer who needs different funding must cancel the Quest and create a new one.
