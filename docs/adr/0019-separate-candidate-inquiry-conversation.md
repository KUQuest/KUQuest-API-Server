# Separate Candidate Inquiry Conversations from Work Conversations

Before a Quest is assigned, a Member who can view the Quest may contact the
Hirer through a private one-to-one Candidate Inquiry Conversation. The
Conversation closes when that Member receives an `ASSIGNMENT_ACTIVE` Assignment
or when the Quest enters `QUEST_ASSIGNED` (or is cancelled before assignment);
it never becomes a Work Conversation and its history is not copied into one.
This keeps pre-acceptance questions private and keeps the Work Conversation
limited to the Hirer and Active Workers.

Closed inquiry content is not visible to Members and follows the applicable
Chat retention and moderation policy.
