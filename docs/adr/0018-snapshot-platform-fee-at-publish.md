# Snapshot the Platform Fee at publish

The Quest captures the `Money Policy` revision when it publishes and reserves Quest Escrow. The Platform Fee is calculated from the net Quest Reward and remains part of the inclusive Quest Funding Total. Completion, cancellation, and dispute settlement use that revision's Platform Fee, so a later policy change cannot make an existing reservation too small or change the Hirer's commitment; the new policy applies only to newly published Quests.
